Should You Use apple2.ianohlander.com or a Dedicated Domain?

This document analyzes whether to host Apple //c Ultra under your personal domain (ianohlander.com) or on a brand new dedicated application domain (e.g. ultra6502.com). It evaluates trademark safety with Apple Inc., SEO domain authority, engineering brand credibility, and monetization conversion rates for a non-marketer.

Strategic Comparison Matrix

Evaluation Criteria Option 1: Subdomain (apple2.ianohlander.com) Option 2: Dedicated Brand (ultra6502.com)
Apple Trademark Safety Extremely High: Clearly identified as an individual engineer's portfolio project under nominative fair use. Requires Care: Must avoid "Apple" in the root domain (use ultra6502.com, NOT apple2ultra.com).
SEO & Backlink Power Maximum Synergy: Backlinks from Hacker News & universities elevate the domain authority of your entire personal site. Starts at Zero: New domain starts with 0 authority and enters Google's 2-4 week sandbox index.

Commercial Scalability Great for Web: Perfect for web demo; desktop sales route to Lemon Squeezy / Steam. Maximum Commercial Autonomy: Allows separate business valuation or standalone acquisition later.

The Recommended Strategy: Launch on Subdomain, Alias to Dedicated Later

Why starting with apple2.ianohlander.com is the smartest commercial and technical move

Phase 1 (Immediate Launch): apple2.ianohlander.com Launch Today

Why launch here first:

  • Zero Delay: You already own ianohlander.com. The CNAME is configured and goes live in minutes.
  • Viral Authenticity on Hacker News & Reddit: Tech communities strongly prefer projects hosted on individual engineers' domains over corporate landing pages. It signals "an engineer built something incredible" rather than a corporate sales pitch.
  • Portfolio Value: When universities or recruiters research Ian Ohlander, having a viral systems compiler under your name establishes immediate authority.
Phase 2 (Commercial Expansion): Register ultra6502.com When Expanding

When you start selling hundreds of desktop copies:

  • Register a clean, trademark-safe product domain such as ultra6502.com or baremetalsilicon.com ($12/yr on Cloudflare / Namecheap).
  • Point the new domain to the exact same GitHub Pages / Hostinger server using a Domain Alias or 301 Permanent Redirect.
  • Users visiting either apple2.ianohlander.com or ultra6502.com will reach the same emulator without losing a single bookmark or backlink!
⚠️ Crucial Legal Rule: Apple Trademarks in Domain Names
Legal Protection

If you decide to register a dedicated domain name, do NOT register a top-level domain containing the trademark "Apple" (e.g. do NOT register apple2ultra.com or apple2emulator.org).

❌ High Trademark Risk (Avoid as Root Domain):
  • apple2ultra.com (Contains Apple trademark in primary commercial domain)
  • apple2workstation.com
  • appleiic.dev

Why: Corporate brand enforcement algorithms routinely file UDRP domain disputes against commercial domains containing "Apple".

✅ 100% Safe Domain Choices:
  • apple2.ianohlander.com (Safe under Nominative Fair Use personal subdomain)
  • ultra6502.com (100% original, memorable, clean-room)
  • baremetalsilicon.com (Matches companion textbook)
  • ultraworkstation.dev
  • retrosilicon.io
Actionable Recommendation for Launch

Launch right now using apple2.ianohlander.com. It is fast, free, 100% safe, builds your personal authority, and keeps your WordPress site on ianohlander.com completely intact. If the desktop app takes off and you want a standalone product brand in the future, you can seamlessly add ultra6502.com in 5 minutes with zero disruption.
