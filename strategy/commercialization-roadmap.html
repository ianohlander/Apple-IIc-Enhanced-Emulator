The Non-Marketer's Business & Financial Glossary

Plain-English Explanations of Core Monetization Concepts (Zero Prior Knowledge Required)

Business 101
1. Merchant of Record (MoR)

What it means: The entity legally responsible for selling goods to the end consumer. Platforms like Lemon Squeezy and Steam act as your MoR.

Why you care: You don't have to hire an international tax accountant. The MoR automatically calculates, collects, and remits sales taxes and VAT in 100+ countries.
2. Sales Funnel (ToFu → BoFu)

What it means: The journey a user takes from first hearing about you to paying you money.

In our model:
• Top of Funnel (ToFu): 100k visitors try the free web demo.
• Middle of Funnel (MoFu): 5,000 read the tutorials.
• Bottom of Funnel (BoFu): 500 buy the $29 Desktop App or $499 Courseware.
3. CAC vs. LTV

CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost): What you spend to get 1 buyer.
LTV (Lifetime Value): Total money a customer pays you over their life.

Our Superpower: Because we use organic viral channels (Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub), our CAC is ~$0, making profit margins extremely high!
4. 90%+ Software Gross Margins

What it means: Unlike physical hardware (which requires raw materials, factories, inventory, and shipping), digital software costs virtually $0 to replicate.

Result: Selling 1 copy or 10,000 copies costs you the exact same server bandwidth ($0 on GitHub/Lemon Squeezy).
5. Net-30 University Invoicing

What it means: Standard business payment terms where an academic institution pays within 30 days of receiving a formal invoice and Purchase Order (PO).

Action: We provide ready-to-fill PDF invoice templates for university procurement departments.
6. The "Open-Core" Commercial Flywheel

What it means: Offering the core emulator freely on the web to earn developer goodwill, while charging for advanced tooling, books, desktop workflows, and institutional packs.

Advantage: Builds a massive community of defenders while commercializing premium assets.
