9. Non-Marketer Plain-English Glossary & Help

Technical definitions for business, tax, and deployment terms

Help / FAQ
Merchant of Record (MoR)

A legal entity (like Lemon Squeezy) that sells your digital goods and handles all global sales tax, VAT, and invoicing on your behalf so you don't have to register in 50 states.

CNAME Record

A DNS record that maps an alias domain name (e.g. apple2.ianohlander.com) to another server host (ianohlander.github.io) without revealing IP addresses.

Statutory Damages (17 U.S.C. § 504)

Pre-established legal damages (up to $150,000 per willful copyright infringement) awarded by courts without requiring the creator to prove actual financial loss.

Nominative Fair Use

A trademark legal doctrine allowing you to use a trademarked name (like "Apple II") purely to describe compatibility without implying sponsorship or endorsement.
