Deep-Dive: How Clean-Room ROMs Execute DSK Images, Type-Ins & Games

The 4 Architectural Layers Enabling 100% Binary Compatibility Without Proprietary Apple Code

A common question in retro systems engineering is: If the emulator uses a clean-room synthesized BIOS instead of Apple's proprietary ROM dumps, how can vintage disk images (.DSK), magazine type-in programs, and complex arcade games run seamlessly?

📐 Figure L.1: Clean-Room Compatibility & Execution Topology ABI & Hardware Layering
1. Unalterable ABI & Vector Jump Tables ($F800–$FFFF)

In 1977, Steve Wozniak established fixed vector entry points for monitor I/O. Any software calling JSR $FDED (Character Out) or JSR $FD1B (Key In) relies on the functional contract (Accumulator contains character, screen updates, registers preserved).

Our clean-room ROM implements this exact ABI contract. The caller doesn't care what byte sequence is inside ROM, only that $FDED prints the character and returns!

2. Disk Images Carry Their Own Operating Systems in RAM

When you boot a floppy (.DSK) or 32MB hard disk, the ROM's only job is executing the 256-byte bootstrap jump at $C600 or $C700 to load Track 0 Sector 0.

The diskette immediately loads DOS 3.3 (RWTS @ $B7E8) or ProDOS 2.4.2 (MLI @ $BF00) directly into RAM ($9D00–$BFFF). From that point onward, the software executes out of RAM, completely independent of the motherboard ROM!

3. 95% of Games Bypass ROM and Write Directly to Silicon

Vintage commercial games (Choplifter, Lode Runner, Prince of Persia, Breakout) never called ROM routines during gameplay because ROM routines were too slow for 60 FPS animation.

Instead, games write directly to hardware softswitches ($C000 Keyboard, $C030 Speaker, $C050-$C057 Video switches, $C0EC-$C0EF Stepper). Because our hardware emulation is cycle-accurate, games run with 100% fidelity.

4. Applesoft BASIC Tokenizer & Zero Page Pointer Mapping

For typed-in or magazine BASIC programs, our engine automatically parses ASCII text into standard Applesoft bytecode tokens ($80–$EA) and initializes standard Zero Page pointers ($67–$68 Program Start, $AF–$B0 Program End).

This allows typed-in programs from Compute!, inCider, and Nibble to run natively without requiring Microsoft/Apple's copyrighted BASIC interpreter ROM binary.

⚡ The "Bring Your Own ROM" (BYOR) Custom Studio Fallback

For rare, esoteric vintage utilities that make undocumented jumps into private, internal subroutines of Apple's 1978 ROM, users can use the built-in ⚡ Custom ROM Studio to flash authentic ROM dumps extracted from their own physical machines with 1 click.

1. U.S. Copyright Registration (Form TX / eCO) Federal Law

Automatic Protection vs. Federal Registration: While copyright exists automatically upon authoring code in fixed form (17 U.S.C. § 102), formal registration with the U.S. Copyright Office is legally required before filing an infringement lawsuit (17 U.S.C. § 411).

Key Benefits of Registration within 3 Months of Publication:
  • Statutory Damages: Eligible for up to $150,000 per willful infringement without needing to prove actual monetary losses (17 U.S.C. § 504(c)).
  • Attorney's Fees: The court can force the infringer to pay your full legal fees (17 U.S.C. § 505).
  • Legal Presumption: Certificate constitutes prima facie evidence of copyright validity in court.
2. Commercial Licensing & EULA Strategy Contract Law

Choose Your Licensing Model: Your license contract defines how others may use, inspect, or license your technology:

  • Dual-Licensing (AGPLv3 / Commercial): Non-commercial open-source users get AGPLv3 (requiring them to open-source all modifications); commercial/enterprise users buy a proprietary commercial license to keep their software closed.
  • Business Source License (BSL 1.1): Source-available for learning and personal evaluation, but strictly forbids commercial SaaS hosting, resale, or institutional deployment without a commercial license.
  • Proprietary Commercial EULA: All rights reserved for courseware packages ($499/dept), digital textbooks, and commercial IDE plugins.
3. Trademark Registration & Distinctive Branding USPTO Protection

Build Your Own Protected Brand: While you use "Apple II" under Nominative Fair Use to describe hardware compatibility, you must register and protect your own unique commercial brand names and logos with the USPTO.

Recommended USPTO Trademark Classes:

  • Class 041 (Education): Providing online courses, university curriculum, textbooks, and interactive labs.
  • Class 042 (SaaS / Cloud): Providing temporary use of online web-based development environments.

Downloadable Desktop Edition: Technical Anti-Tamper & IP Protection Blueprint

How to Package, License & Distribute "Apple //c Ultra Workstation Pro" Without Exposing Proprietary Code

5-Tier Technical Defense

When transitioning from a web-hosted application to a downloadable desktop binary, developers often worry: "If I give users a downloadable build so they can save configurations and custom cards locally, how do I prevent someone from copying my source code or pirating my application?" Follow this 5-tier technical and architectural defense blueprint to ensure the downloadable desktop product remains completely secure, tamper-resistant, and legally protected.

1. Tauri (Rust) Native Packaging Anti-Extraction

Avoid Standard Electron: Standard Electron packages source files into an unencrypted app.asar archive that anyone can decompress with a single command (npx asar extract).

The Tauri Advantage:
  • Web assets and scripts are embedded directly into compiled native C/Rust machine code (.exe, .dylib, .AppImage).
  • 10MB binary size (vs 150MB+ Electron bloat).
  • No loose JavaScript source files on the user's hard disk.
2. WebAssembly (Wasm) Compilation Bytecode Obfuscation

Core Engine Compilation: Lower the 65C02 CPU cycle execution core, MMU memory banking switches, and C#/Java AOT AST compiler lowering engines to compiled WebAssembly (.wasm) modules.

Security Benefits:
  • Variable names, AST node schemas, and control structures are stripped down to raw binary stack instructions.
  • Reverse engineering requires arduous assembly disassembly, giving you industrial-grade IP protection.
3. Offline Ed25519 Digital Licensing Zero-Friction DRM

User-Friendly Offline License Keys: Retro users despise always-online DRM. Instead, utilize asymmetric digital signatures (Ed25519) for desktop license validation.

How It Operates:
  • Upon purchase on Lemon Squeezy/Gumroad, an encrypted license string [UserEmail + Expiry + Signature] is issued.
  • The desktop app verifies the signature offline with your embedded public key—impossible to crack or keygen without your private key.
4. Decoupled User Config & Card Storage Safe Extensibility

Empower Users Without Giving Away Core IP: Store user configurations, floppy disks, and custom virtual peripheral card scripts in an external sandboxed directory (e.g. ~/Documents/Apple2Ultra/).

Architecture:
  • Users write and share their own .card.js peripheral scripts using your public SDK API.
  • The core 65C02 engine and AOT compiler remain private, compiled, and untouched inside the secure application binary.
5. Merchant of Record Distribution Global Compliance

Lemon Squeezy, itch.io & Steam: Distribute the desktop edition through platforms acting as "Merchant of Record" (MoR).

Commercial Safeguards:
  • Automatically handles global sales tax, VAT, chargeback protection, and invoicing.
  • Steam Direct ($100 app fee) provides instant access to millions of PC/Mac retro gaming and software enthusiasts with built-in Steam client anti-tamper.
6. Registered Copyright & Watermarks Legal Recourse

Statutory Legal Armor: Register Form TX with the US Copyright Office within 3 months of desktop release.

Protections:
  • Entitles you to up to $150,000 per willful infringement without proving actual damages.
  • Embedded steganographic author strings prove forensic provenance if anyone redistributes modified builds.
