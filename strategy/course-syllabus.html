Course Overview & Pedagogical Philosophy

CS 340 provides an unvarnished, hands-on journey from high-level modern programming languages (C# and Java) directly to physical microprocessor registers, memory-mapped I/O multiplexers, audio synthesizers, and magnetic mass storage block drivers. Utilizing the zero-install Apple //c Ultra platform, students gain deep intuition for compilation, register allocation, and hardware execution without complex virtualization layers.
