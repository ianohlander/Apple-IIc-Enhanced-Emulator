1. 1-Bit Speaker Pulse Physics & Frequency Math

The onboard Apple II speaker is connected directly to a hardware flip-flop at memory address $C030. Reading or writing this address flips the logic level between 0V and +5V, causing the speaker cone to push out or pull in. Generating a musical frequency \(f\) requires toggling $C030 every half-period \(T/2 = \frac{1}{2f}\) seconds.

Cycle Calculation Formula for Desired Frequency

At standard Apple II clock frequency \(f_{\text{cpu}} = 1.022727\text{ MHz}\), the cycle count per half-period is:

\(\text{Cycles Half-Period} = \frac{f_{\text{cpu}}}{2 \times f_{\text{desired}}} = \frac{1022727}{2 \times f_{\text{desired}}}\)

For Middle C (261.63 Hz): \(\text{Cycles} \approx \frac{1022727}{523.26} \approx 1954\text{ CPU cycles per toggle}\).

2. Dual AY-3-8910 Mockingboard Sound Synthesizer

The Apple //c Ultra integrates a Slot 4 Mockingboard featuring dual General Instrument AY-3-8910 Programmable Sound Generator (PSG) chips driven by dual MOS 6522 Versatile Interface Adapters (VIAs). This provides 6 independent analog tone voices, 2 independent 17-bit pseudo-random noise generators, and 2 hardware envelope generators.

PSG Register Name & Description Width Mathematical Frequency Formula
R0 / R1 Channel A Tone Period (Fine / Coarse) 12-bit (0..4095) \(f_{\text{tone}} = \frac{f_{\text{clock}}}{16 \times \text{Period}}\) (\(f_{\text{clock}} = 1.0227\text{ MHz}\))
R2 / R3 Channel B Tone Period (Fine / Coarse) 12-bit (0..4095) Channel B tone generator
R4 / R5 Channel C Tone Period (Fine / Coarse) 12-bit (0..4095) Channel C tone generator
R6 Noise Generator Period 5-bit (0..31) \(f_{\text{noise}} = \frac{f_{\text{clock}}}{16 \times \text{Period}}\)
R7 Audio Mixer Control (Tone / Noise Enables) 8-bit mask Bits 0-2: Tone Enable (A,B,C); Bits 3-5: Noise Enable (A,B,C). Active LOW.
R8, R9, R10 Channel A/B/C Volume & Envelope Mode 5-bit Bits 0-3: 16-step log volume (0=silent, 15=max); Bit 4: Enable hardware envelope
R11 / R12 Envelope Period (Fine / Coarse) 16-bit (0..65535) \(T_{\text{envelope}} = \frac{256 \times \text{Period}}{f_{\text{clock}}}\)
R13 Envelope Shape / Cycle 4-bit (0..15) Selects Sawtooth, Triangle, Decaying, and Sustained wave shapes
